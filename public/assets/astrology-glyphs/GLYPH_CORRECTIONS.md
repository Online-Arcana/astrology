# Glyph corrections

This revision replaces the original plain-text placeholders.

- Ascendant: interlocking calligraphic `As`
- Descendant: interlocking calligraphic `Ds`
- Midheaven: interlocking calligraphic `Mc`
- Imum Coeli / Nadir: interlocking calligraphic `IC`
- Equatorial Ascendant / East Point: interlocking calligraphic `Eq`
- Vertex: interlocking calligraphic `Vx`
- Mean Node: ascending-node glyph plus `M` modifier
- True Node: ascending-node glyph plus `T` modifier
- Occultation / Solar Eclipse: filled conjunction construction, corresponding to U+1F775
- Lunar Eclipse: filled opposition construction, corresponding to U+1F776

All files are path/geometry based SVGs. They contain no live font text and do not depend on emoji rendering.
